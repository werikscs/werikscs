### Hi, I'm Weriks Santos

Fullstack developer and computer engineer. I work mostly with **TypeScript, Node and React**, and what
interests me most in software is the part that usually stays invisible: architecture that survives
change, tests that make code safe to touch, and delivery that doesn't depend on someone remembering the
steps.

I also design interfaces in Figma — I like being able to take something from the screen design all the
way to production.

Partner at [Darmax](https://darmax.com.br/), where I build systems for clients and our own products.
Currently doing a master's at IFF, with a dissertation on AI agents applied to process automation.
